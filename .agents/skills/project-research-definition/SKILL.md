# Project Research Definition

## Workflow

1. Read `.codex/research_state/research_definition.yaml` and every registered file under `docs/design/`.
2. Separate frozen responsibilities from open scientific questions.
3. Preserve content evidence as the only positive authority, CEG-WM HF direct score as the current raw content-detector candidate, and geometry as conditional recovery.
4. Define the generated medium, model family, controllable components, detector access, attacker capabilities, outputs, failure modes and non-goals.
5. Close root-key/KDF/PRG, LF, routing, combination, Q/K estimation, runtime and
   threshold-selection logic as finite named candidate specifications before construction
   authorization; keep empirical promotion outcomes and calibrated numerical thresholds open
   until governed validation.
6. Update the canonical design documents and outer research-definition manifest together.
7. Keep the 13 implementation responsibilities distinct from the 10 candidate
   registry IDs; combined embedding, LF blind scoring and geometry reliability
   have independent responsibility identities.
8. Give full CEG-WM success, honest negative closure and any reduced-scope method distinct identities and evidence gates.
9. Align the contract stage and planned evaluation surface without adding project implementation.

## Blocking Rules

- Do not implement a method before its primitives and assumptions are documented.
- Do not present threat-model choices as empirical results.
- Do not define success only as an ungoverned headline metric.
- Do not let geometry directly produce a watermark positive.
- Do not allow reference images, embed records or private embedding state in formal detection.
- Do not inherit fixed LF/HF weights or historical-project authority.
- Do not silently change the raw and rectified detector or threshold identity.
- Do not package HF-only plus geometry as full CEG-WM if LF or routing is not promoted.

## Required Validation

- Check that proposed protocols and claims are traceable to the research definition.
- Run the research-definition audit and governance self-tests after contract, manifest or design changes.
