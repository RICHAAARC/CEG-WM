# Research Scope

## Research question

Can a generated image carry a key-specific content watermark whose evidence remains detectable from the ordinary image through the frozen content-adaptive simultaneous LF+HF embedding and its clean joint statistic?

The content-adaptive simultaneous LF+HF embedding and calibrated weighted-joint statistic are the validated content-chain method.

The four positive neutral-counterfactual effects establish only that each frozen allocation input changed the allocation. The two public branch-share population standard deviations can support only that allocation was not identical across the fixed eight-image roster, and only when both independently computed RC0 values are finite and strictly positive. They do not expose or recover masks, tile weights, attention, latents, deltas, or probe state.

Local CPU, fake, and runtime checks remain engineering evidence only. The accepted content-chain result contains four independent physical strata. It does not itself establish paper-level fixed-FPR performance, attack robustness, geometry completion, or paper conclusions.

## Content-chain decision

The content-chain method progression is complete:

1. the keyed HF attribution anchor and LF content statistic remain distinct inputs;
2. correct-key evidence, external wrong keys, and primary-null controls remain separately represented;
3. the public calibration asset freezes the weighted-joint statistic;
4. the formal stability evaluation retains four independent 8/8/32/32 physical strata without pooling;
5. all predeclared units and failures remain in their physical denominators;
6. the accepted formal result completes the content chain without adjudicating deferred geometry or fixed-FPR claims.

The accepted status is `content_chain_method_complete`. This is a content-method conclusion, not a fixed-FPR detector, geometry, robustness, full-system, or paper-readiness conclusion.

## FormalExperiment-V1 scope

The paper experiment contract is now
`FORMAL_EXPERIMENT_CONTRACT_FROZEN / EXECUTION_READY /
EXECUTION_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. Readiness records a closed implementation and
engineering-entry prerequisite; it is not permission to execute a formal
denominator or claim a paper result.

Each of the proposed method and four baselines calibrates its own threshold
from exactly 2,000 clean unwatermarked negatives at target alpha 0.001. The
nearest-rank rule is `k=1998`, `tau=c_(1998)`, with a strict `score > tau`
decision. Attacked negatives never participate in threshold selection; they
reuse the frozen clean-only threshold and report attacked-negative FPR
separately. This clean-only design supersedes any earlier description that
pooled clean and attacked negatives to determine a threshold.

The proposed method uses the current Geometry-Direct/SyncSeal route. Geometry
may provide only reference-frame or coordinate recovery. It does not vote for
watermark presence and is not a reliability gate. Any rectified candidate is
decided by the same content statistic, key semantics, preprocessing, and
content threshold. The formal table contains no rotation-plus-scale condition.

The N=100 proposed-method reconstruction experiment is a supplementary stress
test at approximately 1% negative-sample resolution. It is outside the
five-method main table, is not extended to baselines, and cannot support claims
of comprehensive generative-attack robustness or a reconstruction comparison
across all methods.
