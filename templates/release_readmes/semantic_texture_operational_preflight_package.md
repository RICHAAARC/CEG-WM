an operational, zero-science delivery surface for the implemented but not
scientifically validated semantic-texture candidate.

and all science, threshold, promotion, and claim flags false. It is not a
governed scientific record and does not change the current HF-only formal,
default, or joint detector.

and model bytes. The external bootstrap validates the archive, adjacent
